## Relations where a species would contradict the call

The formula-led frame names a substance. It is offered only where the name
cannot change the answer, because the alternative is a row whose species
contradicts the numbers it prints — a molar mass the call takes as an
argument, a Ka, a coefficient. These are the rest, with the reason.

## Shapes this generator does not carry, and why

| shape | why |
| --- | --- |
| formula-led (a species formula, then conditions) | the relation contract has no species argument, so a row could state a formula it cannot bind to a quantity; 56% of the chemistry source is written this way, and it needs a contract change, not a clause |
| rate, yield or percent as a modifier on a species | same cause: the modifier attaches to a species the call does not take |
| the verbal ask (compare, justify, predict) | 33% of the exam bank asks for an explanation; this architecture refuses it honestly rather than pretending to answer, so it is an abstention example and not a training row |
